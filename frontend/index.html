🎯 Placement Aptitude Challenge

Quantitative Reasoning

A train running at 72 km/h crosses a 200m long platform in 22 seconds. What is the length of the train?

Step 1: Speed in m/s = 72 × (5/18) = 20 m/s.
Step 2: Total Distance = Speed × Time = 20 × 22 = 440 meters.
Step 3: Train Length = Total Distance - Platform Length = 440 - 200 = 240 meters.
